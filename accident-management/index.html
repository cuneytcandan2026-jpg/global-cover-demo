Complete Solutions

We are a leading provider of motor claims and accident management services to the automotive, businesses, private clients and fleet industries. We cover all aspects of claims from the first call through to claim settlement and repair management.

We offer our clients individual solutions based on their needs and business issues. We pride ourselves on our ability to provide the best-in-class service in the market while minimising client costs.

Global Cover offers a new approach to accident management. We believe in providing a high-quality, proactive and responsive service. We have a customer-centric philosophy: supportive and always in partnership.

We manage the claim process from the first customer contact to when a policyholder or driver is back on the road. Services include first response, vehicle recovery and collection services, repair and service deployment, repair management, engineering services, claims management, mobility management and uninsured loss recovery (ULR) services.

Instruct us to handle your insurance claim instead of your insurer.

  • We’ll liaise with insurers on your behalf.
  • We’ll arrange accident recovery if your vehicle isn’t driveable.
  • We won’t charge you an excess if you’re not at fault for the accident.
  • We’ll carry out the repairs using the latest technology and fit vehicle manufacturer genuine parts provided with a lifetime guarantee.
  • We’ll keep you moving with a courtesy or replacement vehicle of a similar size to your own for as long as the repair takes.
  • Whether you’re at the side of the road or you’ve been able to drive home, we can help.
