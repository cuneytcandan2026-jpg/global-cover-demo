New Claim Report

If you have to make an insurance claim, it can be very stressful, especially when your life’s already busy. And we get that, that’s why we’re committed to making the claims experience clearer and easier for you.

Accident Assistance

New emergency motor insurance claims only

02036 594031

Lines are open 24 hours a day ^

Please note: if calling us about an accident outside our working hours, we can only provide emergency assistance to recover your vehicle if it’s undrivable. If this service isn’t required, please call us during office hours.

Accident support for hearing loss customers is provided via the following email address: info@globalcoverltd.com. If you can’t find an answer to your questions, please don’t hesitate to contact us.

Do you have everything you need?

  • Your insurer name, policy number and documents
  • Your vehicle registration number
  • Details of what happened and when
  • Details of any other vehicles, people or property involved
  • Contact details for any witnesses
  • Crime reference number, if applicable

To receive the best possible service, we recommend you call us and speak to one of our highly skilled team who can take a report from you.

Depending on the type of claim you’re making, we may also ask for some photos or videos of the damage. But don’t worry, we’ll let you know how to do this once you’ve told us what’s happened.
